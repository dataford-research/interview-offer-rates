The Worst Tech Interview Experiences in 2026, According to Candidates

By Amney Mounir

Some companies run interviews that candidates really do not enjoy. At Dataford we looked at companies with 200+ rated interview reports and ranked them by the share of candidates who called the experience negative.

The full list of 25 is in our worst interview companies ranking. Here is what the data shows.

The top of the list

  1. Canonical. 63% negative, 12% positive, 8% offer rate. Difficulty 3.44, the hardest on the list.
  2. Airwallex. 47% negative, 20% offer rate.
  3. Oscar Health. 45% negative, 26% offer rate.
  4. Cloudflare. 45% negative, 21% offer rate.
  5. Okta. 45% negative, 25% offer rate.
  6. Navan. 44% negative, 34% offer rate.
  7. Vertafore. 44% negative, 35% offer rate.

Canonical is far ahead. No other company comes within 15 points of its negative rate.

Money does not fix it

Sixteen of the 25 companies pay a median of $180k or more. Well funded software companies make up most of the list, including Cloudflare, Okta, Navan, Braze, GitHub, Gusto, Rippling, Asana and Confluent.

Anthropic is #20 at 38% negative. It has the highest pay on the list at about $555k and the lowest offer rate at 4%.

Most of these loops are not hard

Only Canonical (3.44) and Anthropic (3.28) score above 3.1 on difficulty. Vertafore (2.73) and BILL (2.77) are easier than average and still make the list. Bad experiences come from process: slow replies, unclear steps and ghosting.

GitHub has the highest complaint rate, with 30% of reports describing a concrete complaint, mostly ghosting. Bumble (26%), Navan (24%) and Airwallex (23%) follow.

What to do if you are interviewing at one of these

  1. Ask for the full process up front. How many rounds, who you will meet, and when you will hear back.
  2. Follow up on a set date. If they said a week, send a short note on day eight.
  3. Run other loops in parallel. With offer rates of 8% to 35%, do not pause your search for one company.
  4. Read recent reports first. Our interview experiences show what candidates went through, company by company.

The data comes from 800k+ candidate interview reports in Dataford's pipeline, with known employers only.
